Gilgit-Baltistan

Home to some of the world's highest mountains, including K2, and stunning valleys with unique cultures.

Hunza Valley

A mountainous valley known for its stunning scenery, ancient forts, and hospitable people. Often called "Heaven on Earth".

Major Attractions

  • Baltit Fort - 700-year-old UNESCO site
  • Attabad Lake - Turquoise blue lake
  • Passu Cones - Iconic mountain peaks
  • Eagle's Nest - Panoramic valley view

Best Visiting Months

April to October

Local Culture & Food

Burusho people, famous for longevity. Try Chapshuro (meat-filled bread) and local dried fruits.

Approximate Cost

$30-50 per day (mid-range)

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Known for its lush green valleys, rivers, and diverse cultural heritage.

Swat Valley

Often called the "Switzerland of Pakistan" for its stunning alpine scenery, rivers, and waterfalls.

Major Attractions

  • Malam Jabba - Ski resort
  • Mahodand Lake - Alpine lake
  • Ushu Forest - Pine forests
  • Mingora Bazaar - Local market

Best Visiting Months

May to September

Local Culture & Food

Pashtun culture. Try Chapli Kabab and Swati Pulao.

Approximate Cost

$25-40 per day (mid-range)
