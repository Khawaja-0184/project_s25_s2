Trip Planning Guide

Essential information to plan your Northern Pakistan adventure

Transportation

Options include buses, jeeps, and private cars. For remote areas, 4x4 vehicles are essential.

Accommodation

Choose from hotels, guest houses, and homestays for an authentic experience.

Budgeting

Average daily budget: $30-50 for mid-range travel. ATMs available in major towns.

Packing

Layered clothing, sturdy shoes, sun protection, and altitude medication if needed.
